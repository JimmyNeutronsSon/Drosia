FAQ

Straight
answers.

Something else? Call 425-609-0830 or email hi@verilyclean.net.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Verily Clean is licensed and insured in Washington and carries commercial general liability insurance.

Can you work around active trades?

Yes. We coordinate with your site superintendent and schedule the rough, final and touch-up passes around active trades, inspection dates and handover. Our supervisor-led crews are set up to handle last-minute construction changes.

How large a project can you take on?

We scale crew size to the project and have worked on commercial, multifamily and industrial builds.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Supplies and equipment are included unless your facility specifies otherwise.

Who will actually be in my building?

A trained, supervised crew assigned to your account. The supervisor coordinates the crew, runs the post-job inspection and communicates directly with your team.

Which areas do you serve?

Seattle and the Eastside (Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Issaquah, Bothell), plus Portland and Gresham in Oregon and Vancouver, New Westminster and Port Moody in British Columbia.
